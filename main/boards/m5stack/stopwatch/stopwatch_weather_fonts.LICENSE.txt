StopWatch Weather font subsets
==============================

The generated font data in stopwatch_weather_fonts.c is a modified, fixed-glyph
bitmap subset of Noto Sans SC (Regular 400). The font symbols are named
font_stopwatch_weather_16, font_stopwatch_weather_20 and font_stopwatch_weather_30.
The bitmaps and metrics were extracted without rerasterizing from the installed
78/xiaozhi-fonts 2.0.0 component, using:

  cbin/font_noto_sans_common_16_4.bin
  cbin/font_noto_sans_common_20_4.bin
  cbin/font_noto_sans_common_30_4.bin

Component source: https://github.com/78/noto-fonts
Its manifest.json identifies the Chinese source as
source/Noto_Sans_SC/static/NotoSansSC-Regular.ttf. The 16px subset includes the
update label and printable ASCII; the 20px subset contains controlled Chinese
text, and the 30px subset includes Chinese titles and temperature digits.
Remaining ASCII and punctuation at 20px/30px use the separately linked basic
font fallback. The generator records the source CBIN SHA-256 hashes in the
generated C header.
